# Daily Pulse — experience revision 18

Home now connects understanding, choice and action. A compact daily orientation separates chosen open records, follow-ups due for administrative review and follow-ups scheduled after today. Up to three suggestions use Analytics' existing evidence lanes: one commitment, eligible opportunity review and eligible relationship review first, then available remaining evidence. Already chosen records are excluded. No hidden score, automatic assignment, outreach permission or quota is introduced.

Today uses the existing Focus records and workspace calendar. Choosing a suggestion pins it and selects it for the day through the shared repository. Removing it from Today keeps its pin. Completed follow-ups remain distinguishable from open choices; people, companies and opportunities are not silently declared complete. My Focus remains the place to select arbitrary records and manage all pins. Existing limits and database/backup v6 remain unchanged.

The in-page inspector supports follow-ups, opportunities, people and companies. Inspect/choose moves keyboard focus to the selected heading. Follow-up and opportunity editors use fresh snapshots and existing stale-write checks. Completion uses the existing transactional path; the inspector retains the completed record so the user's context does not disappear. Rescheduling leaves the explicitly chosen priority in Today. Future scheduled work is disclosed with dates, not labeled unimportant. Saved profile navigation and context remain available.

A daily wrap-up uses current saved follow-up completion dates and actual conversation occurrence dates in the selected timezone, excluding notes and future entries. Reopening a task removes it from completion totals. Historic/import creation dates, XP, inferred relationship quality and fabricated stage history do not determine these counts. Visible evidence lists are bounded to five per kind and full history remains in Activity. Quiet days have an intentional empty state; no quota or completion reward is added.

Personal setup leads with one next foundation and an optional full checklist, including a real opportunity editor. Completion reflects current saved records and a backup download request, not proof of retained backup. Hiding/reopening the guide and existing Help paths remain intact. Empty Personal shows actual create/import actions rather than zero-value performance charts. Optional progress, pinned relationships, recent local reminders and the selected Focus landing preference remain available. Focused Home puts chosen work before suggestions; Compact reduces spacing. Show My Focus on Home controls the chosen list without deleting pins or daily selections.
